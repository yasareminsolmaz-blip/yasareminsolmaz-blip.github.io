Reelpark – Car Video Player

Support

Reelpark plays your own videos on iPhone and, while the car is parked, on the CarPlay screen over AirPlay.

Why don't I see video in my car?Video on the CarPlay screen needs a car whose system supports AirPlay video, and it only plays while the car is parked. Other cars play the sound only.
Which videos can I add?Videos from Files, Photos, your computer (Finder file sharing) and direct links to MP4/MOV files or HLS streams (.m3u8). Websites like YouTube are not supported.
How does the free trial work?Tap “Start 7-day free trial”. After 7 days you can unlock Reelpark with a one-time purchase. There is no subscription and nothing is charged automatically.
I reinstalled or changed phones.Open the purchase screen and tap “Restore purchases”, signed in with the same Apple ID.
